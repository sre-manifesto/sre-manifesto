### About this e-book

This ebook introduces the core ideas behind AI, machine learning, and generative AI from an engineering perspective. It focuses on the practical implications for DevOps, DevSecOps, platform engineering, and site reliability engineering.

The goal is to help SREs, platform engineers, and software engineers understand when AI/ML is useful, where it adds operational value, and how to start adopting it responsibly in production environments.
